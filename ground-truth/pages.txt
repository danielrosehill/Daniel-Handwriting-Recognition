=== P15 ===
Good handwriting takes patience, and
mine has never been especially neat.
At school the teachers complained that
my letters leaned in every direction.
These days I mostly type, so when I do
pick up a pen my hand gets tired after
a page or two. Still, there is something
about writing on paper that helps me
think more clearly. The slowness forces
me to choose words more carefully, and
crossing things out leaves a record of
how the idea changed along the way.

=== P16 ===
Recipe: simple lentil soup
1 cup red lentils, rinsed
1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 cloves garlic
1 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt
1.5 litres of water or stock
Fry the onion until soft, about 5 min.
Add carrots, garlic and the spices.
Stir in the lentils and the water.
Simmer for 25 minutes, then blend.
Squeeze in half a lemon at the end.
Serves four. Keeps for three days.
Freeze the rest in small portions.
